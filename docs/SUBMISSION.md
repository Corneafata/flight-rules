**Project Description** (paste as is)

Flight Rules is a browser game that trains young students to think like NASA flight controllers. Players pack a 10,000 kg lander for a four-person Moon outpost: solar panels, batteries, a water-wall storm shelter, a greenhouse, oxygen, water and food. Then they write up to six IF/THEN flight rules that run the outpost automatically. The outpost flies hour by hour through a real record of the 2023 lunar day and night at Chandrayaan-3's landing site, built from NASA JPL Horizons and the real 1 September 2023 solar particle event in NASA's DONKI database. When the outpost fails, the debrief names the "first domino". Students fix one rule and re-fly the identical record, so they see exactly what their decision changed. Every number in the game is labelled as observed, published or game estimate.

**Which NASA Data Source Are You Using?**

NASA/JPL Horizons API (Sun and Earth elevation at the Chandrayaan-3 landing site, Aug–Oct 2023); NASA CCMC DONKI (solar energetic particle event of 1 Sep 2023 and solar flares); NASA-STD-3001 technical brief OCHMO-TB-002 (crew oxygen, water and food needs). Also ISRO Chandrayaan-3 mission timeline (partner agency) and the Chang'e-4 lunar radiation measurement (Zhang et al. 2020).
